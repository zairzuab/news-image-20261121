Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: a front page, 1024×1024, with
the nine biggest stories of Saturday 21 November 2026, each with its own
photograph.

- **Lead:** Missiles rain on Kyiv — and land in Poland. Russia launches its
  biggest barrage of the year — 400 drones and 110 missiles — killing eight
  in Kyiv and crippling bridges and power; two missiles come down in Poland,
  and Warsaw calls it a deliberate provocation.
- **Markets:** AI rout drags the S&P 500 into correction; the Nasdaq erases
  all its 2026 gains.
- **Justice:** Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide — the World Court finds a
  state guilty of genocide for the first time in history.
- **Climate:** COP31 runs past its deadline as talks in Antalya stall over
  fossil fuels.
- **Europe:** UK and EU sign their biggest post-Brexit deals — food trade,
  carbon markets, youth mobility.
- **Tech:** Anthropic debuts as a $2 trillion company — the biggest stock
  debut in history raises $81.3bn.
- **Cyber:** FBI database hacked as US outages spread.
- **Health:** Cancer pill makes tumours vanish in trial — daraxonrasib leaves
  tumours undetectable in one in 12 pancreatic cancer patients.
- **Peru:** 6.9 quake shakes the Peruvian Andes.

An "Also today" strip adds breadth: Trump tells Europe to defund Ukraine,
Houthis fire on Riyadh for a third night, Spain heads to a 29 November
election, Gen Z protests in 25 countries, Bayer pays $7.25bn over Roundup.

The photographs are AI-generated illustrations of the reported events. The
program holds the finished image as `entry.png` and copies it to
`/output/entry.png` (no network, deterministic — both runs write the same
bytes).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
